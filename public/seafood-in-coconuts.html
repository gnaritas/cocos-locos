The signature · Lakeland FL

Seafood in coconuts

Split a real coconut in half and two things happen: it becomes a plate, and it becomes a cup. That is the heart of Cocos Locos — seafood that arrives in the shell and cocktails poured into one.

Ask the shop Late-night bar

How it works

The shell does the serving

Owner Kris Cavazos built the concept on a simple move: coconuts are split in half, and some of the seafood dishes are plated right in the halves — cold aguachiles and seafood cocktails are the natural fit, with the broad seafood section (fried fish included) stretching across the menu beside them.

Which dishes come in coconuts is part of the menu reveal at opening — the team is testing recipes now, and the lineup is announced when the kitchen is happy with it.

The drinks side

The same coconut, filled with a cocktail

The other half of the coconut gets the bar’s share: cocktails served in coconut cups. Order one at the bar or bring one to the table with your seafood.
