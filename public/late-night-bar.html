The weekend flip

Same room, two lives

Through the day and weeknight evenings, Cocos Locos runs as a family-friendly, full sit-down restaurant. Friday and Saturday, the plan changes: the bar stays pouring until 3 a.m. for the crowd coming from and back from events at the Corral Event Center nightclub next door — Cavazos built the late-night hours around exactly that traffic, before and after the shows.
