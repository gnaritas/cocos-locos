The signature

Coconuts on the table, coconuts in the bar

Owner Kris Cavazos built Cocos Locos around a simple idea: split real coconuts in half and use them as plates for some of the seafood dishes — and as cups for cocktails.

The kitchen

Good questions

Before you ask

When do you open?

Opening soon — the opening date has not been announced yet. Email the team and they will answer with the latest news.

Why serve food and drinks in coconuts?

It is the house tradition owner Kris Cavazos built the concept around: real coconuts split in half — plates for some seafood dishes, cups for cocktails. Which dishes come in coconuts will be revealed with the menu at opening.

Is it family-friendly?

Yes — through the day and evening it is a family-friendly sit-down restaurant. On weekends it shifts into a late-night spot for guests of the Corral Event Center next door.

Where exactly is it?

At 1313 N Combee Road in Lakeland — the remodeled space of the former Pa’Las Miches Bar and Grill, about 176 seats.

Is there a full menu yet?

Not published — the team is testing recipes now. The full menu and prices will be announced at opening. The shop posts first looks on its Facebook page.
