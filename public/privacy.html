Privacy

What this site does — and does not

The contact form

The form on the contact page sends the name, phone, email, and message you type straight to the restaurant’s inbox by email. Nothing is stored on this site itself — the form is a post office, not a database.

No cookies, no trackers, no scripts

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party scripts. There is no advertising here.

The map

Pages showing the location embed a Google Maps frame. When your browser loads that frame, Google handles the request under its own privacy policy and may set its own cookies; this site passes nothing to Google.

Questions

Ask by email at cocoslocosfl@gmail.com or through the contact form — whatever reaches the shop.
